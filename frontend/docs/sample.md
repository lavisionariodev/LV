An online app for funeral services in the Philippines could offer a modern, convenient, and transparent way for families to plan and manage final arrangements. The platform would feature a tiered system of packages and a la carte services, all automatically covered by a robust insurance policy to guarantee service delivery.
Here is a detailed list of services, from basic to complete, with descriptions:

Tiered Packages
These packages simplify the decision-making process by bundling essential services at various price points. Every package is automatically covered by an insurance policy to ensure the services are delivered as agreed upon.

1.Basic Package: Direct Disposition This is the most economical and straightforward option, ideal for families who want to fulfill the final rites with minimal ceremony and expense.
Direct Cremation: This service includes the retrieval and transport of the deceased from the place of death (home, hospital, etc.) to a licensed crematorium. The package covers all necessary permits, the cremation process itself, a simple, basic urn for the ashes, and the return of the remains to the family. It specifically excludes any form of embalming, public viewing, or memorial service.
Direct Burial: This service includes the transport of the deceased to a pre-selected cemetery. It covers a simple, standard casket and the burial process, including securing the burial permit. This package does not include embalming, a wake, a funeral mass, or a hearse.

2.Standard Package: Traditional Filipino Wake and Service This package provides a traditional and complete funeral service, allowing families to hold a wake and a formal ceremony to honor their loved one.
Traditional Cremation Service: Includes embalming and cosmetic preparation of the body, a wake held in a funeral chapel or at home for a specified number of days (e.g., 3-5 days), and a memorial service before the cremation. This package also includes a standard urn and a rental casket for the viewing period.
Traditional Burial Service: Includes embalming and cosmetic preparation, a wake in a funeral chapel or at home for a set number of days, a standard metal or wooden casket, and the use of a hearse for the funeral procession. The service also covers a funeral mass or service and the graveside committal.

3.Complete Package: All-Inclusive & Personalized Life Celebration This premium package is designed for a seamless, stress-free, and highly personalized experience. It covers all aspects of the funeral and interment.
Customizable Life Celebration: Includes all the features of the Standard Package, but with significant upgrades and added services. This can include:
oPremium Casket or Urn: A wider selection of high-end caskets or decorative urns.
oExtended Wake Duration: A longer period for the wake (e.g., up to 7 days).
oEnhanced Floral Arrangements: Custom floral designs and a more elaborate memorial setup.
oCatering and Refreshments: Food and drinks for guests during the wake.
oProfessional Photography & Videography: Documentation of the funeral service and wake.
oFuneral Program and Obituary: Professional design and printing of memorial cards and assistance with publishing the obituary.
oLive Streaming of the Service: A dedicated and secure live stream of the wake and funeral service, perfect for relatives abroad. This would provide a private link for family and friends to view the service in real-time, with an option for a recorded copy.
oAssistance with Legal and Administrative Matters: The app would provide a checklist and guidance for claiming benefits (e.g., SSS or GSIS funeral benefits) and securing necessary documents (e.g., death certificate).


4.Other Separate Services (À la Carte)
These services can be purchased individually, allowing users to customize their arrangements or supplement a package.
Purchase of Memorial Lot: The app would feature a marketplace for memorial lots (cemetery plots or niches in a columbarium) from accredited memorial parks. Users could browse options, view photos, and purchase lots directly through the app.
Live Streaming for Relatives Abroad: A crucial and timely service. The app would offer a private and secure live streaming feature, complete with a unique link for guests, a recorded copy for future viewing, and possibly a digital guestbook for online condolences.
Embalming and Body Preparation: For families who are handling their own wake arrangements.
Casket and Urn Catalog: A marketplace within the app to browse and purchase a variety of caskets and urns.
Chapel Rental: Booking a chapel for a wake for a specified duration.
Hearse and Transport Services: Arranging for a hearse and other funeral vehicles.
Crematorium Services: Standalone cremation service for those who have other aspects of the funeral already planned.
Grief Counseling: Referral to professional counselors or online support groups
.
5.Automatic Insurance Policy and Service Guarantee
Every service selected through the app, whether a package or an à la carte item, would be automatically covered by a third-party insurance policy. This is a crucial trust-building feature that addresses a major concern in the pre-need industry.
Pre-Need Plan Protection: The app would partner with a licensed and regulated pre-need company, such as those regulated by the Insurance Commission of the Philippines. This ensures that the funds paid for the services are held in a trust fund and are protected.
Service Delivery Guarantee: The insurance policy guarantees that if the funeral provider selected through the app is unable to deliver the contracted services (e.g., due to closure, natural disaster, or other unforeseen circumstances), the insurer will either:
1.Arrange for a comparable service: The insurer will find a different, accredited funeral home to provide the exact same services and products as outlined in the original contract.
2.Provide a cash payout: If a comparable service cannot be arranged, the insurer will pay the monetary value of the service to the beneficiary, allowing them to make their own arrangements.
Anti-Inflationary: The policy ensures that the services are provided at the pre-paid price, regardless of how much funeral costs may have increased over time.
Simplified Claim Process: The app would provide a clear and easy-to-follow process for the beneficiary to file a claim and receive the benefits, minimizing stress during a difficult time.
